The Transformers universe centers on Cybertronians—sentient, transforming machines powered by Energon and driven by a life force known as a spark. Across many continuities, their history reaches back to ancient cosmic forces like Primus and Unicron, or artifacts such as the AllSpark and the Matrix of Leadership.

At the heart of the franchise is the long-running civil war on Cybertron between the heroic Autobots, led by Optimus Prime, and the conquest-driven Decepticons, led by Megatron. When energy dwindles or the conflict spills off-world, battles frequently pull Cybertronians to Earth, where they adopt disguised alternate modes and clash alongside or against human allies.

Because Transformers spans decades of cartoons, comics, films, and games, specific origin stories and character roles vary across continuities—from Generation 1 and Beast Wars to later animated and live-action timelines. That mix of recurring factions, iconic figures like Bumblebee and Starscream, and shifting continuity details makes it great fodder for testing lore recall and spotting model hallucinations.
